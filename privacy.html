Portfolio

This policy explains what happens to information you share with Gurwinder Singh ("I", "me") through this portfolio website (the site). It is a personal site: there is no account system, no login, and no membership.

1. Information you give me

When you use the contact form or send me an email, you may give me:

  • your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message;
  • anything else you choose to include in that message.

The contact form runs entirely in your own browser. It checks that the fields are filled in, then hands the message to your email application. The site itself does not transmit, store or log your message, and nothing you type is sent to me until you press send in your own mail app.

I do not ask for and do not want payment card details, government identifiers, health information, or any other sensitive personal data. Please do not send it.

2. Information I publish

My email address, phone number and city are printed on the site so that recruiters and clients can reach me directly. That information is public by design; if you would rather it were removed, ask and I will take it down.

3. Google Fonts

This site loads two typefaces — Anton and Poppins — from Google's font service at fonts.googleapis.com. When your browser fetches them, your IP address and browser details are passed to Google, which may log them under its own privacy policy. Google Fonts does not set advertising cookies in this setup.

I chose this because it is simple and keeps the site fast. Hosting the font files on this domain instead would remove the Google request entirely; ask me and I will do that.

4. Cookies, local storage and tracking

This site sets no cookies and uses no local storage, no analytics, no advertising or social trackers, and no fingerprinting. There is no consent banner on this site because there is nothing non-essential to consent to.

  • Essential files only: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, images and the 240 frames of the scrolling background animation, all served from this domain.
  • No Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar, LinkedIn Insight or similar script is embedded.
  • No third-party embeds, chat widgets or embedded maps that would set their own cookies.
  • No email addresses are harvested in the background and nothing is shared with advertising networks.

If this ever changes — for example if I add analytics or connect the contact form to a form-processing service — this section will be updated before the change goes live, and where the law requires it, a consent banner will appear before any non-essential cookie or tracker is set.

5. Server logs kept by my hosting provider

Like any website, the host that serves these files records ordinary request data — your IP address, the page and file requested, your browser's user agent and the timestamp — in server logs for security, abuse prevention and troubleshooting. That is done by <HOSTING PROVIDER> under its own privacy policy, and I do not control it. I do not read those logs for marketing, and the IP addresses in them are not added to any list or shared with anyone.

6. Why I use the information

  • To reply to enquiries about work, roles, freelance deployments or support.
  • To keep the site available and fix errors.
  • To decide what to write about, based on what visitors actually read.

Where the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (India) applies, the lawful basis is my legitimate interest in responding to professional enquiries, or — for a message you send me first — your consent in sending it. You can withdraw that consent at any time by asking me to delete it.

7. Who I share it with

I do not sell, rent or trade personal data. Information leaves my own systems only in these cases:

  • Your email provider — when you email me, the message passes through your mail service and mine (Google Gmail).
  • The hosting provider — for the server logs described in section 5.
  • Google — for the two web fonts described in section 3.
  • Sites you choose to visit — LinkedIn and GitHub Pages links open third-party sites with their own policies.

I do not send data to anyone else, and I do not use automated decision-making or profiling.

8. How long I keep it

  • Enquiry emails: kept while the conversation is relevant, then removed from the active inbox. A short archive of work-related correspondence may be kept for up to 24 months.
  • Server logs: kept only as long as the hosting provider's operational policy requires (typically 30 days), after which they are deleted automatically.

9. Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of the information I hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • stop using it, or restrict how I use it;
  • receive it in a portable, machine-readable format;
  • object to my processing of it.

Email me and I will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority — in India, the Data Protection Board; in the EU or UK, the authority for where you live. Where a request can be satisfied only by editing hosting logs I cannot access, I will explain that limit honestly rather than pretend the record was deleted.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. The contact form is processed in your browser, so what you type is not stored on any server I control. I keep no database of visitor details and no copies of identity documents. No system is perfectly secure, but no sensitive data is collected here in the first place.

11. Children

This site is a professional portfolio and is not directed at anyone under 18. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes to this policy

If I change what the site collects or how I use it, I will update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be mentioned in my case studies or social profiles.
